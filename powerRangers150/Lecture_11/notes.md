# Javascript

What is hoisting? // Not hosting. 

- Whenever you are trying to access a variable or a function even before it's declaration then that concept is known as hoisting.

Types of hoisting:
1. General Hoisting. (= var)(only)(Undefined)

2. Temporal dead zone. (DTZ) (= let and const)(error)
    * In the case of DTZ you will get a special error "cannot access a variable before initialization"
